Component            Pin Name       ESP32 Pin      Notes / Resistor Requirements
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Water Level Sensor   SIGNAL (AOUT)  GPIO 34        ESP32 ADC1 Pin (Analog Input)

Green LED            Anode (+)      GPIO 25        Through 220Ω - 330Ω resistor
                     Cathode (-)    GND            Common Ground

Yellow LED           Anode (+)      GPIO 26        Through 220Ω - 330Ω resistor
                     Cathode (-)    GND            Common Ground

Red LED              Anode (+)      GPIO 27        Through 220Ω - 330Ω resistor
                     Cathode (-)    GND            Common Ground

Active Buzzer        Positive (+)   GPIO 14        Audible critical alert (<= 15%)
                     Negative (-)   GND            Common Ground


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ANALOG WATER LEVEL SENSOR CALIBRATION DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The water level sensor outputs a variable analog voltage proportional to the
depth of water contacting the sensor traces or probe plates.

ESP32 12-bit ADC converts this into a raw value between 0 and 4095:
- DRY SENSOR (0% Water Level)     : ~500 ADC (SENSOR_MIN_RAW)
- MAX SUBMERGED (100% Water Level): ~2800 ADC (SENSOR_MAX_RAW)

Conversion Formula:
waterLevelPct = ((rawADC - SENSOR_MIN_RAW) / (SENSOR_MAX_RAW - SENSOR_MIN_RAW)) * 100
